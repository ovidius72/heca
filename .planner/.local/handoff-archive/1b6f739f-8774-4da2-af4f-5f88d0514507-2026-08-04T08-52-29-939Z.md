## 🔴 Next: T388 — the palette's rows become composed children

**This is the task the user asked for, twice, and got frustrated about — read it in full before
writing anything.** `Label::wrap` and `Label::marks` exist but **nothing on screen uses them**,
because the palette hand-draws its row text. That is the whole point of T388.

Two facts already established, so do not re-investigate:
1. **Overlay layers are laid out every frame** — `heca/src/chrome/mod.rs:2285` `layout_layers` runs
   `LayoutEngine::compute` on each visible layer root at viewport size. Palette children will get
   real engine-measured bounds. This was the one thing that could have sunk the approach.
2. **Follow `Select`, not `Dialog`.** `Select` (`heca-grid-ui/src/widgets/select.rs:188, :365, :554`)
   is an overlay whose rows are real children, measured in its own flow and then shifted into the
   panel at absolute targets. Dialog positions its panel *through layout*, which the palette cannot
   do: `TOP_FRAC`/`BOTTOM_FRAC`/`PANEL_VIEWPORT_FRAC` are viewport fractions and padding/margin are
   `f32` px, so keep `layout()` computing the panel rect as it does now.

Consider `Item` as the row: it already owns the active pill, the `ActiveMarker` accent bar and the
hover signal — three of the things the palette hand-draws — and its slot setters are `host_only`, so
they take composed children.

The two traps are in the task: rows must be **hidden, not rebuilt** as the query filters (`Select`
never faces a changing row count), and the first frame needs a font-derived fallback height because
measured heights come from the previous layout pass (`select.rs:532`).

## Facts worth not rediscovering

- **The SDK drift guard demands a setter for every `#[prop]`** — `heca-view-realize`'s
  `every_widget_property_is_reachable_from_the_sdk`. It fired for `wrap` and `mark_color`; the fix is
  a builder in `heca-view/src/build.rs`. A `host_only` prop (a list, a composed value) is exempt —
  that is why `marks` needed none.
- **A label inside a `Flex` resolves to font 13.5, not 15** — size-variant scaling. A test that
  hardcodes 15 to compute the monospace cell measures the box a character wide, and will accuse
  correct code of overflowing. Read `base().font` *after* layout.
- **Taffy asks three width questions** and a wrapping label has three answers: definite width wraps
  into it, `MaxContent` is one line, `MinContent` is the longest word. Collapsing them makes a label
  in an auto-sized parent measure one line and paint three.
- **`font::wrap_indexed` is the single wrapping implementation** and returns each character's source
  index; `wrap_lines` is derived from it, and `Label::drawn_indexed()` applies the cut and the reflow
  in one place. That index is what lets a mark survive either transformation — counting through the
  drawn output drifts, because `Ellipsis::Start` shifts every surviving character and wrapping
  collapses whitespace.
- Handoffs live on `phase.handoff`; `.planner/HANDOFF.md` is deprecated. `resume.json`'s `nextSteps`
  are stale free text — read the task.
- `planner-task-complete` fails on an unticked checklist; `force: true` is the way when the work
  shipped without the checklist being ticked.
