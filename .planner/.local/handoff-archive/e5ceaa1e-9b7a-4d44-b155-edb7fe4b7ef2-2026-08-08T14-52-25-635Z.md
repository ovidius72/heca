## What was being done
The phase began after P058 Toast/ToastStack foundation work. P061 then completed the app-owned notification domain and configuration: extensible sources, lifecycle/defaults, runtime IDs and canonical model, Intent-backed actions, severity mapping, config semantics, draft construction, and the thin ToastSpec projection.

The final model keeps queue, deduplication, lifecycle timing, history, and dispatch ownership in Heca; grid-ui receives only presentation data and opaque host targets. `NotificationAction` stores a name-keyed `heca_view::Intent` with arguments, never a closure or only a `WmAction`.

`NotificationSystem::App` uses ToastStack; `System` currently falls back to ToastStack with one warning per session until P063; `None` suppresses presentation but allows stderr/debug logging. `notification_history_limit` is runtime-only and not persisted.

## Recent decisions
- Notification actions store `heca_view::Intent`, not closures and not only `WmAction`.
- Severity is app-owned and maps exhaustively to grid-ui (`Error → Danger`).
- Toast projection carries only id, title/body, severity, label, dismissibility, and host-owned opaque `HintTargetId`s.
- Queue, lifecycle, timer, deduplication, history, and dispatch remain app-owned.
- `NotificationSystem::System` is an in-app fallback until P063; `None` suppresses presentation but not stderr/debug logging.
- Runtime notification history is not persisted.
