## Preservation constraints

- **The column parent move is the OTHER half of T474 and is UNSTARTED.** It was tried once as one lump and reverted; 1765 tests stayed green through every defect. **Four traps, all still there:** (1) flex stacking drops the gap between panes, so a pane's header lands on its terminal text — place children at the slots `columns_with_positions` gives; (2) moving placement skips the per-frame `shell::focus_state_to` write, so the active pane loses its highlight; (3) a pane's key sits on the `Pane` **inside** its `KeyHint` wrapper, so a naive `base().key` lookup finds nothing and every pane is rebuilt every frame (100% CPU idle); (4) panes are painted individually in render.rs Pass 3 under a per-pane theme, so a column painting its children either flattens them to one accent or double-paints.
- ⚠️ **Ownership and painting cannot be separated** — the column's `paint_child` walks its children, so the moment panes are children they are painted twice. A first attempt at "ownership only, painting unchanged" was wrong for that reason, and its replacement — a host lookup `pane::shell(state, id)` — was a registry by another name and was reverted too.
- **Additive first, sweeps last.** C1–C5 were built so nothing breaks and no call site is forced to change; that is why the sweep is separable at all.
- **The wire moves only deliberately.** `"gap"`/`"gap_spacing"` and `pad_spacing_*` stay as described props until the SDK half is designed.

## Recent decisions

- **A capability is one builder taking any spelling of its value.** `Length` and `Space` both: a number, a string, or a named constant, with one parser and the variant never written at a call site.
- **All sixteen `*_boxed` twins go at once**, not one widget at a time — that is how a library ends up half-converted.
- **The app assigns what a letter MEANS; the widget only places it.** `accent`/`warning`/`success`/`muted` is a vocabulary this app owns, so `DockFrame` takes `fold_hint_tone` rather than choosing `Muted` itself; the placement stays in the widget, because only it knows where its chevron is.
- **Naming a scope takes a target OUT of the ordinary picker** — the direction matters, because a ⊠ that deletes something must not wear a letter in the picker you navigate with. A picker matching nothing letters **nothing** (fail closed).
- **`KeyHint` is only for a region that is not a widget you can put a builder on.** Its paint only paints its child; the letter is drawn by `paint_child` for anything carrying one.
- **Rejected:** `hintable(false)` on the column as a quick fix — it gates every *collected* picker, so it would also hide the column from a plugin's own. Rejected: `Width::Full` as a separate enum — `Length::FULL` needs no new type and reads on a height.
