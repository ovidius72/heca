## Preservation constraints

- **The column parent move is still unstarted**, and it is what T474 was opened for. It was tried once as one lump and reverted; 1765 tests stayed green through every defect. **Three traps remain, and one is gone:**
  1. Flex stacking the panes DROPS THE GAP, so a pane's header lands on its terminal text. Place children at the slots `columns_with_positions` gives — the WM owns pane geometry.
  2. Moving placement skips the per-frame `shell::focus_state_to` write, so THE ACTIVE PANE LOSES ITS HIGHLIGHT. Nothing else writes it.
  3. A pane's key sits on the `Pane` INSIDE its `KeyHint` wrapper (`chrome/pane/shell.rs:162`), so a naive `base().key` lookup finds nothing and every pane is REBUILT EVERY FRAME (100% CPU idle). Resolve through the wrapper — or delete that wrapper the way the exposé card's was.
  4. ✅ **GONE.** It read: panes are painted under a per-pane theme, so a column painting its children flattens or double-paints. The cause was the theme-swap tinting; a pane publishes its hue with `.accent` now, so painting is one walk and a column may own and paint its panes.
- **Ownership and painting still move together.** The column's `paint_child` walks its children. "Ownership only, painting unchanged" was tried and was wrong; its replacement, a host lookup `pane::shell(state, id)`, was a registry by another name and was reverted too. Do it in steps Antonio drives one at a time — four commits, each verified, is what worked after the revert.
- **A widget says its own pick.** `on_hint`, `hintable`, `hint_placement`, `hint_tone` are on every widget. `KeyHint` is only for a region that is not a widget you can put a builder on. Six wrappers remain in the app and are candidates for removal — see "What is left".
- **The theme is always first.** `.accent` is an override on top of it, like `fill`/`border`/`glow`.
