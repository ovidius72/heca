## Open decisions — Antonio raised these and they are NOT settled
- **Ordering inside a region.** He asked for `append()` / `add_at(index, widget)`. Today order comes from the provider's `default_order`, which is the child deciding its position among its siblings — the same inversion `Region::child` removed for the parent.
- **Should a region derive its own template?** He asked for it to be automatic. My argument against, unanswered: deriving `1fr 1fr` from "there are two children" is counting children to decide the arrangement, which is what T517 exists to delete. A region with no template stacking by share is the safer default.
- **Providers carrying their own grid placement.** `.area(..)`, `.row(..)`, `.column_span(..)` are on every widget now, so a container could say where it sits and the region only say the shape. Agreed in principle, not built.
- **Composition vs registry for regions.** Antonio settled that a registry is fine, since a caller must ADD to a region that already exists rather than build a new one. `Region::child` is that. The app's own sidebar is still assembled through it rather than as a composed tree.
